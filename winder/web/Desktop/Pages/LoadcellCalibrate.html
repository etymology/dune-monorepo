Load Cell Calibration

Hang a known weight from the load cell, wait for the reading to settle, and capture a sample. Each sample pairs the raw tension_tag with the weight converted to newtons. The fit degree is the lowest the samples support (capped below), so the PLC math stays simple; unused coefficients are written as zero. The lever has a dead zone below ~1.5 N and saturates near ~11 N, so calibrate within that working range and leave the intercept free.
